# Tekknote Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Tekknote does not collect, store or share your personal data. It has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no Tekknote server.

**On your devices.** Your notes and recordings stay on your devices. Transcription happens on your device; only Apple's speech files are downloaded, and your voice is not sent anywhere.

**Permissions.** The microphone is used to record notes. Reminders access is used only when you add a note to Reminders. You can turn either off in Settings.

**Where your data can go, at your choice.** If you turn on sync, your notes, recordings and device name are saved to the folder you choose, under that folder's provider's terms. Anything you share or export goes where you send it. Your device or iCloud backups may also include Tekknote's data.

**Apple.** Apple may give me crash reports and usage statistics from people who agree to share them. During the beta, TestFlight may also give me your name and email, install and session counts, crash reports and any feedback you send. Apple's own privacy policy covers this.

**Deleting your data.** Delete notes in the app, or delete the app to remove what it stores on that device. Copies in a sync folder, exports or backups are yours to remove.
